Questions before you book

How do I get an estimate?+

Tell us what space needs attention, where it is in Las Cruces, and when you would like service. The public business profile says estimates are free; the team can confirm the scope directly.

Can I book recurring cleaning?+

The public service listing mentions one-time, weekly, biweekly, and monthly options. Ask about current availability and the cadence that fits your space.

Do I need to provide supplies?+

You can mention any product preferences or surfaces that need special care in your request. Confirm supply details with the team when your visit is arranged.

Can I request a custom task?+

Yes, describe what you need. The team can confirm whether it is available, how it would be scoped, and whether it is separate from a standard clean.

Do you serve nearby towns?+

Las Cruces is the confirmed location. If you are outside Las Cruces, include your town in the request so the team can confirm service availability.
